## Steady pressure, lasting results

Braces work by applying gentle, continuous pressure that moves teeth through the bone over time. They remain the most reliable way to treat complex crowding and bite problems.

## Ceramic braces

Ceramic braces offer the perfect balance between effectiveness and aesthetics. They work just like traditional metal braces but use clear or tooth-coloured brackets that blend in with your teeth.

## What to expect

1. **Records.** A 3D scan, photographs and X-rays to plan tooth movement.
2. **Fitting.** Brackets are bonded and the first wire placed in about an hour.
3. **Adjustments.** Short visits every six to eight weeks.
4. **Retention.** Fixed and removable retainers protect the result.
