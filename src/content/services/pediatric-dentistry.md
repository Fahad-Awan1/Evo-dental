## Happy first visits, healthy habits for life

Children who enjoy the dentist become adults who look after their teeth. Our pediatric team moves at your child's pace, explains everything in kid-friendly words and celebrates every small win.

## What we look after

- **First check-ups** from the first tooth or first birthday.
- **Preventive care** such as fluoride varnish and sealants on back teeth.
- **Gentle fillings** for baby teeth when decay does occur.
- **Early orthodontic checks** around age seven to guide jaw growth.

## Tips for parents

Keep talk about the visit positive and simple, bring a favourite toy, and book a morning slot when little ones are at their best. We will handle the rest.
