## Straighter teeth without the brackets

Invisalign uses a series of custom-made clear aligners to gently shift your teeth into perfect alignment. Each set is worn for one to two weeks, then swapped for the next.

## How it works

1. **Digital scan.** No putty impressions, just a quick 3D scan.
2. **Your plan.** We show you an animation of how your teeth will move.
3. **Wear.** Aligners are worn 20 to 22 hours a day and removed to eat and brush.
4. **Check-ins.** Brief visits every eight to ten weeks to keep everything on track.

## Is Invisalign right for me?

Aligners suit most mild to moderate crowding, spacing and bite issues. For complex movements, braces can be faster. A consultation will tell you which is the better route.
