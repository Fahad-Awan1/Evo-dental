## Small visits that prevent big treatments

Most dental problems are painless at the start. Regular check-ups let us find a weak spot in the enamel or early gum inflammation while it is still simple, quick and inexpensive to treat.

## What happens at a preventive visit

1. **Conversation first.** We ask about any sensitivity, changes or worries since your last visit.
2. **Examination.** Teeth, gums, bite and soft tissues are checked, with low-dose digital X-rays if they are due.
3. **Hygiene.** Plaque and hardened tartar are removed, then teeth are polished so they feel glassy smooth.
4. **Your plan.** You leave knowing exactly how your mouth is doing and when to come back.

## Who is it for?

Everyone. Adults usually benefit from a visit every six months. If you have gum disease, diabetes, braces or a dry mouth, we may recommend coming a little more often.
