## A brighter smile, safely

Coffee, tea, red wine and time all darken teeth. Professional whitening uses a dentist-strength gel that breaks stains apart inside the enamel without removing or damaging it.

## In-clinic or at home

- **In-clinic power whitening.** Gums are protected, gel is applied in short cycles and you leave noticeably brighter the same day.
- **Custom home trays.** Made from a scan of your teeth and worn for a short time daily over two weeks, for gradual, controlled whitening.

## Good to know

Whitening does not change the colour of crowns, veneers or fillings, so we plan around existing dental work. Some temporary sensitivity is normal and settles within a day or two.
