## The closest thing to a natural tooth

An implant is a small titanium post that acts as an artificial root. Once the bone has bonded to it, a custom crown is attached, giving you a tooth that is fixed, strong and easy to clean.

## The three parts

- **Implant.** A titanium screw placed gently in the jaw under local anaesthetic.
- **Abutment.** A connector that joins the implant to the crown.
- **Crown.** A porcelain tooth shaped and shaded to match its neighbours.

## Your timeline

1. **Consultation and 3D scan** to check bone and plan the position.
2. **Placement**, usually under an hour for a single implant.
3. **Healing** for three to six months while bone integrates.
4. **Final crown** fitted and adjusted to your bite.

Most patients compare the procedure to having a filling and return to work the next day.
