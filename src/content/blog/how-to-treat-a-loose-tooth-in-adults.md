Loose teeth are part of childhood. In adults, they are a sign that something is affecting the support around the tooth, and the sooner it is checked, the better the chance of keeping it.

## Why adult teeth become loose

- **Gum disease.** The most common cause. Infection slowly breaks down the bone and ligament that hold the tooth.
- **Injury.** A knock from sport or a fall can stretch the ligament.
- **Grinding or clenching.** Constant heavy forces, often during sleep, can loosen teeth over time.
- **Pregnancy and hormonal changes.** These can temporarily affect the tissues around teeth.

## What to do straight away

1. Leave it alone. Avoid wiggling it with your tongue or fingers.
2. Eat soft foods and chew on the other side.
3. Keep the area clean with gentle brushing and a salt-water rinse.
4. Book a dental appointment within a few days, or the same day after an injury.

## How dentists treat it

Treatment depends on the cause. Deep cleaning can halt gum disease and let tissues tighten. A loosened tooth can be splinted to its neighbours while it heals. A night guard protects teeth from grinding. If the tooth cannot be saved, an implant or bridge can replace it.

## The takeaway

A loose tooth rarely fixes itself, but it is far from a lost cause. Early treatment makes all the difference.
