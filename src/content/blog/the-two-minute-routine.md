Two minutes, twice a day. It sounds easy, but the average person brushes for about 45 seconds. Here is the routine we follow ourselves.

## Step by step

1. **Pea-sized fluoride toothpaste** on a soft or electric brush.
2. **Angle at 45 degrees** towards the gumline, where plaque gathers.
3. **Small circles, light pressure.** Scrubbing wears gums and enamel.
4. **Thirty seconds per quarter.** Outer, inner and chewing surfaces.
5. **Spit, don't rinse.** Leaving a film of fluoride protects teeth for longer.

## Clean between your teeth

A brush reaches only about 60% of each tooth. Use floss or interdental brushes once a day, ideally before your evening brush.

## Common mistakes

- Brushing straight after acidic food or drink. Wait 30 minutes.
- Keeping a brush too long. Replace it every three months.
- Forgetting the tongue, where odour-causing bacteria live.

Small improvements, repeated daily, are what keep you out of the dental chair.
