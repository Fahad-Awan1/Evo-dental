It is the question we hear most in orthodontic consultations. The honest answer is that it depends on your teeth and your routine.

## Clear aligners

**Best for:** mild to moderate crowding or spacing, and adults who want a discreet option.

- Nearly invisible and removable for meals
- Easier brushing and flossing
- Fewer emergency visits, no broken wires
- Need discipline: 20 to 22 hours of wear a day

## Braces

**Best for:** complex bite corrections, rotated teeth and patients who prefer not to manage wear time.

- Work around the clock with nothing to remember
- Handle difficult movements efficiently
- Ceramic brackets make them far less noticeable
- Some food restrictions and more careful cleaning

## Cost and time

Treatment time is similar for comparable cases, typically 6 to 24 months. Fees are also closer than most people expect, and both can be spread over monthly payments.

## How we decide together

A 3D scan lets us simulate both options. You will see the expected result, the timeline and the cost side by side before choosing.

Whichever you pick, retainers afterwards are what keep your smile straight for life.
